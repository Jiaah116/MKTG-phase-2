# TeamFit Stakeholder Walkthrough Guide

Use this guide when a project, account, operations, or agency manager agrees to discuss how they review additional client requests. This is a discussion guide, not a record of findings. Record only what the participant actually says or does after the walkthrough.

## Materials

- [TeamFit public demo](https://teamfit-phase-2-prototype.vercel.app)
- Fictional request cases and staffing information: **To be shared by the test organizer.**
- Discussion notes or response form: **To be shared by the test organizer.**

Use fictional or appropriately de-identified information only. Do not enter confidential client requests or employee records into the public demo.

## 1. Learn about the current process

Ask about a recent additional or changed client request:

1. How did the request arrive?
2. What did you need to check before responding to the client?
3. Which tools did you use, such as email, chat, a spreadsheet, or a project-management tool?
4. What made the request difficult to scope or staff?
5. What happened when information, an estimate, or staffing was wrong or incomplete?

## 2. Walk through TeamFit

Show two fictional requests.

1. **Spring launch graphics with missing information**
   - Show the original request and unresolved details.
   - Prepare and edit the hold note.
   - Distinguish client questions from internal agency checks.
   - Record the hold without guessing a deadline or effort estimate.

2. **Complete request with a potential candidate**
   - Show the confirmed request details and aggregate capacity check.
   - Explain why the person is a potential candidate.
   - Explain that this is not an assignment, schedule confirmation, or client commitment.
   - Show that the manager must check schedules, dependencies, leave, and deadline feasibility.

## 3. Ask for feedback

Ask the participant:

1. Where, if anywhere, would this workflow fit into your current process?
2. Was it clear what the client needed to answer and what the agency needed to check internally?
3. What does “Potential candidate” mean to you after seeing the screen?
4. What information would be hardest to keep current?
5. What would stop you from using this alongside your current tools?
6. Who would use this day to day, and who could approve a limited pilot?
7. What outcome matters most: clearer client follow-up, fewer revised commitments, less rework, faster response, or something else?

## 4. Record evidence responsibly

For each walkthrough, the organizer should record:

- Anonymous participant label, role, and industry or agency type
- Current process and tools described
- Observed reactions and direct feedback, clearly marked as participant statements
- Confusing steps or objections
- A possible design change or test implication
- Limitations, including whether the participant belongs to the target creative or marketing agency segment

Do not claim that TeamFit saves time, improves profitability, or would be adopted unless the participant's actual feedback supports that conclusion. After the walkthrough, record real findings in a separate stakeholder-findings evidence file; do not replace this guide with invented results.
